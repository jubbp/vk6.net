# A new vk6.net is on the way

We're rebuilding vk6.net. The new site brings every NewsWest episode and its show notes together in one place, with a player that works on your phone, a searchable archive, a map of where NewsWest is broadcast, and one calendar for club meetings, nets and contests.

The old addresses will keep working. If you spot something wrong or missing, email [newswest@vk6.net](mailto:newswest@vk6.net).
