The Amateur Radio Disposals Helpline is a weekly broadcast of pre-loved equipment for radio amateurs, shortwave listeners and electronics enthusiasts, and for members seeking help. It has run for 40 years or more.

## Getting your items on air

If you have items for sale, or you're looking for equipment or information, contact Roy VK6XV and he will do his best to help.

- Email: [vk6xv@bigpond.com](mailto:vk6xv@bigpond.com), with "Helpline" in the subject
- Deadline: **Thursday morning** for the following Sunday's broadcast
- Plain text please, nothing fancy: it saves hours of editing.

This week's Helpline items are in the [latest episode's show notes](/news/listen/).

73, Roy VK6XV
