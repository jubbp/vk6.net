Record any or all of these scripts and send them to [newswest@vk6.net](mailto:newswest@vk6.net).

**Tips for success**

- Record the script exactly as written. Where it shows something in curly brackets, like {name} or {callsign}, use your own first name and callsign.
- Record as MP3, 256 kbit/s mono, or the best quality your device can do.
- One audio file per script.
- Name the file with the script's name below, then a full stop and your callsign.
- We recommend the free audio tool [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/).

## VK6ARN WA and Beyond.{callsign}

> Covering Western Australia and beyond, this is NewsWest, brought to you by WA Amateur Radio News, on air, online and on demand. This is VK6ARN and I'm {name}, {callsign}.

## NewsWest on Sunday.{callsign}

> I'm {name}, {callsign}, and I listen to NewsWest on Sunday {mornings, evenings} on {160, 80, 40, 2} metres via VK6ARN.

## Since 1931.{callsign}

> Since 1931, Amateur Radio News has been broadcast in Western Australia. NewsWest is brought to you by WA Amateur Radio News, on air, online and on demand. This is VK6ARN and I'm {name}, {callsign}.

## Not Amateur Radio News.{callsign}

> If it's not Amateur Radio, it's not Amateur Radio News. NewsWest is dedicated to Amateur Radio for Amateur Radio Operators. This is VK6ARN and I'm {name}, {callsign}.
