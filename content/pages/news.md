The main VK6 NewsWest broadcast is every Sunday morning at 09:30 WST (01:30 UTC), following the 09:00 WIA National News. It goes out through the linked repeater network, which starts at VK6RAP on 146.700 MHz, and on several HF frequencies. NewsWest is also relayed in VK2, VK3, VK4, VK7 and VK8: see the [full broadcast schedule](/news/broadcasts/) for your area.

The National News and NewsWest are repeated from 19:00 WST (11:00 UTC) on Sunday evenings.

## Callbacks

Please listen on air and give your callsign at the end of the broadcast as a callback. It tells the NewsWest team how many people are listening, and the numbers are reported to the WIA. If you can't call in on air, [log your callback online](/callbacks/).

## Can't listen on air?

Every episode is available to [play or download](/news/listen/), and as a podcast: search for "NewsWest" in your podcast app or [subscribe](/subscribe/).

NewsWest is produced by a small team of volunteers. To contribute, [submit your news](/news/contact/) or [record one of our scripts](/news/scripts/). Report any problems with downloads to [newswest@vk6.net](mailto:newswest@vk6.net).
