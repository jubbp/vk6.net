Amateur radio news has been broadcast in Western Australia since the early 1930s, almost without a break. For most of that time it was made by whoever was willing to do it next. That was its weakness, and also its strength: the news was always made by people who wanted to make it. Somewhere along the way the broadcast took the name **NewsWest**, and the name has stuck.

## Milestones

- **Early 1930s:** amateur radio news broadcasts begin in WA. The club's records put the start in 1930 or 1931.
- **Before 2014:** Bob VK6POP takes over NewsWest from Dennis VK6KAD (SK), first as a few months' help while Dennis recovered from illness. Dennis didn't recover, and Bob carried on. A call for helpers to produce an episode of the National News draws in more people, and the idea of a club begins to form.
- **2014:** a group of amateurs meets to form an association whose main purpose is a news service for amateur radio in WA, with education and activities written into its constitution.
- **20 January 2015:** WA Amateur Radio News Inc is incorporated. It becomes a WIA affiliated club.
- **2015:** WAARN activates **VI6ANZAC** around ANZAC Day and **VK100ANZAC** during August, as part of the WIA's ANZAC centenary campaign. A team takes part in the John Moyle Field Day and Remembrance Day contests. In November, **NewsHound 101**, our first course, teaches amateurs to write, record and edit news items.
- **2016:** the first **PerthTech**, a one-day technical seminar in Perth, is held in April. It becomes a regular event.
- **2018:** a second NewsHound course, in Bassendean.
- **2019:** NewsWest episodes are kept online; the archive on this website starts here.
- **2022–2024:** PerthTech moves to the Gidgegannup Recreation Club, with caravan and camper parking and a sundowner.
- **April 2023:** WAARN supports **VI6CRO** at the Carnarvon Space and Technology Museum for the total solar eclipse. [Read more](/vi6cro/).
- **Late 2025:** after more than a decade producing NewsWest, Bob calls on WA's amateurs to keep the news going. A new team forms to share the work, taking turns as producer.
- **2026:** NewsWest becomes a podcast, the archive moves to new hosting, and vk6.net is rebuilt.
