PerthTech is WA Amateur Radio News's technical day for amateur radio operators: a full day of presentations, from the purely technical to practical stories from people's own experience, followed by a sundowner.

## What a PerthTech weekend looks like

- **Friday afternoon and evening:** informal activities for those arriving early.
- **Saturday:** the main programme, starting at 8:30 am, with morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea, and the raffle and door prizes through the day. Presentations are recorded on video and audio.
- **Saturday evening:** a sundowner for those who want to stay on.
- **Sunday morning:** a BBQ breakfast and informal show-and-tell before everyone heads home.

Caravan and camper sites are available on site for the Friday and Saturday nights, so you can stay for the sundowner without worrying about the drive home.

The most recent PerthTechs were held at the **Gidgegannup Recreation Club**, about 17 kilometres up Toodyay Road from Roe Highway. Registration has always been required in advance, so catering can be planned.

## History

PerthTech began in **April 2016** as a one-day seminar in Perth, with presentations and demonstrations by amateurs skilled in particular subjects, and became an annual event. The 2020 event was cancelled.

## The next PerthTech

PerthTech has always been paid for mostly by a big raffle, drawn on the day, alongside the ticket sales. Last time the raffle fell short of what the event cost, and the club covered the gap. We'd love to run PerthTech again, but not by betting the budget on raffle sales.

That's why we're building a PerthTech fund through **WAARN Supporters**: if enough listeners and clubs chip in a little each year, the next PerthTech can be booked and planned with confidence, and the raffle goes back to being a bit of fun on the day. [Find out how you can help](/support/).

Have an idea for a talk or a demonstration, or want to help on the day? Email [perthtech@vk6.net](mailto:perthtech@vk6.net).
