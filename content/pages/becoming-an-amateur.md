If you're interested in getting an amateur radio licence, there are several ways to go. Local WA clubs can help you with training and assessment. It's not difficult to get started: a weekend is often all you'll need. See the [VK6 clubs](/clubs/) page for clubs that offer training.

## Licence levels

- [Foundation](https://www.wia.org.au/licenses/foundation/about/)
- [Standard](https://www.wia.org.au/licenses/standard/about/)
- [Advanced](https://www.wia.org.au/licenses/advanced/about/)

## Free resources

- [Foundation Licence Study Guide](https://vkradioamateurs.org/flsg/) (free)

## Myths

**Do I need to learn Morse code?** No, not since 2005. You don't even need to learn the phonetic alphabet, though it's a good idea to know it.

**You can't do much with a beginner's, or Foundation, licence.** Not true. You can talk to the world with the power you have as a beginner, often with even less. You can experiment with your gear and antennas, take part in club activities and much more.

**It's expensive.** It can be, but it doesn't need to be. You can get started with a $50 radio. Course costs vary depending on where you train: about $25 for a syllabus and $75 for the licence itself. After that you can spend as much or as little as you like.
