NewsWest has gone to air every week for decades, made entirely by volunteers. Running it costs money: hosting every episode back to 2019, the podcast feed, this website, the vk6.net domain, and the equipment our producers use. Events like PerthTech cost more again.

**WAARN Supporters** is how listeners, clubs and businesses can keep it all going. A small yearly contribution from enough people covers our running costs completely, and builds a fund so we can bring back PerthTech without the money worries.
