Getting in touch with the News Team is simple: email [newswest@vk6.net](mailto:newswest@vk6.net). The team will receive it and one of us will reply, often within the hour. Please remember we're all volunteers.

## What to send

Send your story as **audio** if you can: it brings your voice and your club to air. You can also send **text**, and a member of the News Team will read it on your behalf. If you have a late-breaking story that urgently needs to go to air, email us and we'll try to fit it in.

## Recording tips

- Write it down first and read it through aloud once before recording.
- Record somewhere quiet, with soft furnishings, close to the microphone.
- Say your name and callsign at the start or end.
- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) is free and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux; it's what we use.

## Checklist

- The date of the broadcast it's intended for
- Audio as MP3, 256 kbit/s, mono
- Your contact details
- Emailed to [newswest@vk6.net](mailto:newswest@vk6.net)

## Learn to produce for radio

We offer a course on producing material for broadcast: writing an audio script, recording it and editing the result. [Email us](mailto:newswest@vk6.net) to ask about the next one.
