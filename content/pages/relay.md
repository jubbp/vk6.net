If you'd like to broadcast NewsWest in your area, you can. There are no restrictions, other than that you broadcast it **as supplied, without any modification**. Please tell us you're transmitting the news, so we can list you on the [broadcast schedule](/news/broadcasts/) and include you in notifications.

## Getting the audio

The current episode is always at the same address on [audio.vk6.net](http://audio.vk6.net/), named by its broadcast date: `http://audio.vk6.net/YYYYMMDD.newswest.mp3`. It is published before the Sunday broadcast. The address and file name pattern don't change, so scheduled downloads keep working.

## Broadcaster notifications

Registered broadcasters can receive an email when each new episode is published and when its show notes change.
