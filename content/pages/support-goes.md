## Where your support goes

- **Keeping NewsWest on air and online:** audio hosting and the archive, the podcast feed, the website and the vk6.net domain.
- **Equipment and software** for the volunteers who produce the news each week.
- **PerthTech:** a fund to bring back our one-day technical seminar in Perth, with presentations and demonstrations from amateurs who know their subject, so it can be planned with confidence and kept affordable for everyone who comes.
- **Training:** courses like Newshound101 that help amateurs write, record and produce for radio.

The committee reports what supporters' contributions paid for at each annual general meeting.

## A note for businesses

NewsWest is broadcast on the amateur radio bands, which can't be used for commercial purposes. So businesses are **never mentioned on air**, and sponsorship can't buy airtime. We're glad to thank our sponsors everywhere else: on this website and at PerthTech.
